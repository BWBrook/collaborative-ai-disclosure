# Roadmap

This is an editable backlog, not a fixed sequence of twelve principles. Topic 6 is where the founding discussion stopped; it was resolved on 2026-10-23 (D010). Later topics below are proposed continuations.

## First tranche: stabilise the draft

Review conceptual fidelity against the founding discussion; examine the licensing and public metadata defaults; rerun mechanical checks; and turn the [issue seeds](issues/README.md) into selected GitHub issues after the repository exists. Human review of source summaries is pending.

## Resume the conceptual discussion

**6. Verification and scientific warrant.** Resolved: origin tells you which checks a contribution needs; warrant decides whether it stands. The operational account is in [warrant and independence](docs/warrant-and-independence.md) and is under review.

**7. Evidence for governance under delegation.** What distinguishes substantive scientific evaluation from nominal endorsement when AI performs most execution?

**8. Proportionate disclosure.** What belongs in a short paper declaration, in Methods and in the extended account without making every project complete three forms?

**9. Record uncertainty and preservation.** What minimum account is reasonable for older projects, partial conversations, multiple model generations and protected records?

**10. Truthful policy adaptation.** How should the framework handle conflicting requirements and differences between its collaboration vocabulary and venue terminology?

**11. Usability across workflows.** Test real, permissioned cases from several disciplines, including bounded assistance, intertwined collaboration and changing modes. Examine whether the categories illuminate rather than flatten the process.

**12. Community stewardship.** Decide how proposals, dissent, versions and policy updates should be maintained, and whether a methods paper or commentary would be useful.

These later topics were not all posed or endorsed in the original conversation. Reorder, combine or replace them as the project develops.

## Before calling this a stable release

Resolve or deliberately document the main conceptual disagreements, complete human review of material source claims, test template usability on actual permissioned cases, check current target policies, and state limitations. A green repository check alone is not a release criterion for a scientifically or ethically mature framework.

## Deliberately deferred

A public website, automated policy monitoring, model-execution services, quantitative contribution scoring and a declaration-generation application are not part of the first scaffold. None is required for people to use the prose templates.
