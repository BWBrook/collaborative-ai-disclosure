# Test predominant modes and narrative-first descriptions

The four modes and their overlap were welcomed as useful framing. Their utility across disciplines has not been demonstrated. Use several permissioned actual cases, or initially clearly labelled fictional cases, without assigning a labour percentage.

Test whether two readers can understand the consequential relationship from the headline mode plus narrative; whether mode changes over time can be described naturally; and whether authors feel pressured to claim human-only conceptualisation or writing. Record cases that do not fit rather than forcing them into an existing label.

Compare with related frameworks, including AIdIT, after reading the actual definitions rather than relying on an abstract. Optional interoperability must not quietly make role decomposition compulsory.

**Completion:** document example disclosures, users' observed difficulties and concrete revisions. Do not claim validation or community endorsement from a small convenience exercise.
