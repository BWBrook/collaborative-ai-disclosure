## D010 adoption record

**Date and venue:** 2026-10-23, in a Claude Code desktop-app session on Barry's WSL laptop.

**What was put:** Claude's [first-pass review](reviews/2026-10-23-claude-first-pass.md), section 2, proposed the proposition with three supporting points. Warrant governs confidence. Origin still matters epistemically, because different generators fail in characteristic ways, so origin determines which checks a contribution needs. Origin also matters separately for credit, rights and accountability, which belong to the authorship and responsibility statement.

**Barry's reply, verbatim:** "Adopt the topic-6 proposition as stated. Record it in the decision log and proceed with the warrant-and-independence draft."

**How the reply was produced:** the text matches, word for word, a reply option that Claude had drafted and offered in the app's next-step box. Barry selected it rather than typing his own wording. The decision is his; the words were drafted by Claude.

**Scope of the adoption:** the proposition. The operational account of checks (independence, power and the classes of check) was not part of what was put to Barry, and it remains a proposal under D016.

## Changing a decision

Identify the decision being changed, the reason and consequences, and whether it changes an established direction or an unendorsed proposal. New evidence or a better conceptual account can justify revision. The purpose of preservation is traceability, not to make the founder's early views immune from criticism.
