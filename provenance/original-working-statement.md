# Original working declaration

**Historical draft, not a ready-to-submit certification.** This is the two-paragraph statement proposed in the initial answer and welcomed by Barry as representative of his workflow. Later discussion refined the philosophy; detailed verification criteria remain unresolved.

The wording is preserved to retain the useful starting point. In particular, its broad assertions about reference checking, computational evaluation and final decisions must be substantiated for each project. Their presence here does not establish that these activities occurred in a particular study.

> Generative and agentic AI systems, principally OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex, were used extensively and iteratively throughout this project. Their roles included research ideation and critical discussion; literature and data discovery and synthesis; development, testing, debugging and refinement of computational workflows; exploration and critique of analytical approaches and interpretations; and planning, drafting and revision of the manuscript. AI use occurred through extended human–AI interactions rather than isolated prompts, and contributions therefore cannot meaningfully be assigned sentence-by-sentence, line-by-line, or idea-by-idea.
>
> AI-generated suggestions and outputs were treated as provisional rather than authoritative. References and literature-derived claims were verified against source material; computational outputs were evaluated through execution, testing, diagnostic checks and reproducible analyses; and proposed interpretations were assessed against the data, existing knowledge and the authors' scientific judgement. AI suggestions were frequently modified or rejected during this process. The human authors made all final decisions concerning the research design, analytical methods, interpretation, inclusion of material and submitted manuscript, and accept full responsibility for the integrity and conclusions of the work. Further details of the AI-assisted workflow, including tools, approximate periods of use, research roles and verification procedures, are provided in the accompanying reproducibility repository.

## What later discussion adds

The narrative should not imply that the humans personally performed or pre-approved every operation. Missing records and multiple model generations need explicit treatment. The categories should allow overlaps. Role descriptions should be optional aids, not a rigid assignment scheme. The acceptance and verification language must remain grounded in what actually happened.
