# Example: bounded assistance

**FICTIONAL. Not a report of an actual project.**

## Illustrative declaration

AI use was limited to investigating an error in a plotting function and suggesting clearer wording for two figure captions. The researchers tested the revised function against the expected output and checked the final captions against the figures and analysis. The AI did not participate in the project's question formation, data interpretation or substantive argument. The tool, recorded version and date of use are identified in the accompanying software note. The authors approved the resulting changes and take responsibility for the published work.

## What this example is testing

Limited support does not need to be called embedded collaboration. Equally, the statement about what AI did not do would be misleading if it had also participated elsewhere. This example only works when the stated limits are true. The amount and placement of required disclosure remain journal-specific.
