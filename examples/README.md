# Fictional worked examples

All examples in this directory are **invented demonstrations of wording**, not disclosures from real papers or claims about Barry's research. Named checks are fictional and must never be copied as if they occurred in another project.

[Embedded collaboration](embedded-collaboration.md) illustrates an integrated workflow and incomplete historical records. [Bounded assistance](bounded-assistance.md) shows that brief, limited support can be described without inflated collaboration language. [Delegation and exploration](delegation-and-exploration.md) tests the boundary between the other two modes without claiming present autonomous-science capability.

These examples are for testing the usefulness and honesty of the framework. They are not journal-approved declarations. Their verification language is illustrative and predates the operational account in [warrant and independence](../docs/warrant-and-independence.md).
