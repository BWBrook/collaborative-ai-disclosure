# Licensing

**Scaffold defaults for maintainer review before public release.** The founding discussion requested public reuse and contribution but did not select licences. The choices below are proposed in this local package; publication under them should follow the maintainer's review. No public release has occurred as part of scaffold construction.

Copyright, where applicable, 2026 Barry W. Brook and contributors. No ownership is asserted over third-party materials, or over elements for which copyright does not arise.

## Proposed scope

| Material | Proposed licence |
| --- | --- |
| Original explanatory prose, framework documentation and decision records outside the exceptions below | [CC BY 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt) |
| Original reusable content in `templates/` and fictional examples in `examples/` | [CC0 1.0](LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt) |
| Code in `scripts/` and `tests/`; `.github/` configuration; `.gitignore`, `.gitattributes`, `.editorconfig`, `repo_config.json` and `CITATION.cff` | [MIT](LICENSES/MIT.txt) |

CC0 is proposed for the copy-and-adapt passages so that reusing declaration wording is not designed to impose a framework-attribution sentence inside a paper. A citation to the framework is welcome when discussing its intellectual contribution, but is not represented here as an extra condition on CC0 material. The scope does not transfer rights in any third-party content a user later inserts into a template.

## Third-party material

Publisher policies, articles, product names, quotations and linked materials retain their own rights. The repository stores concise original summaries and source links rather than redistributing full articles or policy pages. The original licence texts are included with their own notices unaltered except that the MIT copyright notice identifies the project rights holders.
