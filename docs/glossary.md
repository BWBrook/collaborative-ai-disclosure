# Working vocabulary

**Collaborative workflow:** an iterative relationship in which contributions and responses change the developing research. A functional description, not a claim about machine consciousness or formal authorship.

**Predominant mode:** the interaction category that best summarises a project's overall relationship, qualified by other modes where needed.

**Governance:** substantive direction, judgement, evaluation and integration of research, distinct from personally executing most operations. The evidence required to substantiate governance remains under development.

**Operational execution:** the doing of tasks such as processing data, running analyses, writing implementation code or formatting outputs. It can require intellectual skill; “execution” does not mean trivial or unimportant.

**Disclosure:** a communicated account of relevant AI involvement. It may be short and venue-specific.

**Documentation:** records sufficient to explain a workflow more fully, including its uncertainty and limitations. Not all retained documentation need be public.

**Provenance:** the available account of where materials and claims came from and how they developed. It may be partial and must not be presented as complete when it is not.

**Deliberative record:** preserved observable discussion, instructions, objections and decisions. This does not refer to inaccessible model-internal reasoning.

**Clean research release:** a focused, versioned research package that generates and explains the reported outputs without irrelevant development clutter. It must retain information necessary to interpret the findings.

**Scientific warrant:** the evidence and reasoning that justify a claim, conditional on its assumptions and limitations. Under P06, warrant decides whether a claim stands; origin indicates which checks it needs.

**Shared pathway:** something a check has in common with the process that produced what it checks: model, context, specification, framing, data or code. A possible common cause of error, not a measured dependence. Proposal under D016.

**Oracle:** how the expected answer of a check was established, for example an analytic solution, planted parameters, a cited passage or a reviewer's judgement. Proposal under D016.

**Source check:** examination of a specified source for a specified purpose at a recorded time. Finding a title, reading an abstract and checking an actual policy clause are different levels of evidence.

**Scaffold proposal:** material introduced while constructing this repository, not an opinion already endorsed in the founding discussion.
