## Editing cautions

Do not use “independent” merely because a second model reviewed the output. Do not say “all references were checked” when only bibliographic existence or a sample was checked. Do not imply that a complete transcript or development history exists unless it does. Some journals require additional details or different placement; the narrative should remain truthful when adapted.
