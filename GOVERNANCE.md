# Project governance: initial draft

The proposed initial maintainer is Barry W. Brook, who supplied the research practice, motivation and substantive responses underlying the framework. This is a scaffold arrangement for his review, not a claim that a wider governing body already exists.

Contributors may propose changes through issues and pull requests after the public repository is created. Conceptual changes should identify the affected decision and rationale. Strong criticism is compatible with preserving the discussion record: the record explains how the project developed, not which views must remain permanent.

The maintainer should distinguish routine editorial changes from modifications of the framework's commitments. Significant disagreements and resolutions should be retained in the issue or decision history. Future arrangements for co-maintainers, review responsibilities, conflicts of interest, moderation and succession remain open.

No institution, journal, publisher, workshop participant, AI provider or professional society is represented as endorsing this project. Public participation and potential scholarly publication are goals, not accomplished facts.

A confidential reporting route has not been established in this scaffold. Do not post secrets, private allegations or sensitive source material in public issues. Establish an appropriate private contact mechanism before inviting such reports.
